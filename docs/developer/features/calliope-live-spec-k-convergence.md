## Goal

The Atelier is the one way to make a Quilltap theme, and the documentation
says so. At the end of this spec:

- `@quilltap/theme-storybook` and `create-quilltap-theme` are gone from the
  repository, from the release workflow, from lint and from every live
  document;
- nothing the app or its checks need has gone with them;
- the help pages that exist are true of the app without the two packages;
- the proposal is closed: every open question has an answer or a named
  place on the roadmap.

## Out of scope

The theme author's guide (decision 6: necessary, and not part of this
release); any tool, frame or headless render by which Calliope sees the rendered app
(decision 2); layout variants; `.css` as a native
text type in database stores; foundry-9/quilltap-server#228; any change to
the bundle format, the engine, the registry or the hook catalogue; rewriting
historical documents (release notes, `docs/CHANGELOG.md` history, specs in
`features/complete/`).

## Where things stand

**`@quilltap/theme-storybook`** (1.0.124) is a Storybook preset with 20
hand-written stories under `src/stories/components/` and two stylesheets.
Nothing in the repository depends on the package. It publishes from
`release.yml` (job `publish-theme-storybook`).

One of its files is used by the app's own tooling. The compiled
`packages/theme-storybook/src/css/qt-components.css` (563 KB, generated by
`npm run generate:storybook-css`, held current by
`scripts/check-storybook-css.mjs` in `npm run lint`) is the first entry of
`STYLED_BY_SOURCES` in `lib/themes/assist/styled-by-build.ts`. Calliope's
styled-by table is built from it, because every `@apply` in it is already a
declaration.

**`create-quilltap-theme`** (3.0.0) scaffolds a version 2 bundle directory
from `templates/bundle`. `npx quilltap themes create <name>` runs it through
`npx`, which the Docker image does not have
(`docs/developer/docker-test-plan.md`, row 8.3). A unit test
(`__tests__/unit/packages/create-quilltap-theme.test.ts`) holds its output
valid and its `HOOKS_VERSION` equal to the catalogue's. It is published by
hand.

**What replaced them.** The specimen gallery draws every catalogued hook
from the app's own components, with a coverage test. The Atelier creates a
draft blank, as a copy of an installed theme, or from a `.qtap-theme` file,
and installs and exports it.

**Documentation.** `help/theme-editor.md` describes the Atelier section by
section. `help/themes.md` and `THEME_DEVELOPMENT.md` still open with
`npx create-quilltap-theme`. No document walks an author from start to
finish.

## Appendix — outline for the author's guide (not built here)

Kept so the later work starts from it. A start-to-finish walk through the
Atelier, in the house voice, for someone who has used Quilltap but never
opened a theme, linking to `help/theme-editor.md` for the detail of each
section. A new help page (suggested `help/making-a-theme.md`,
`url: /calliope`) is the likely form; that is for the later work to
settle.

1. Starting: a blank draft, a copy of a theme you like, or a `.qtap-theme`
   file someone sent.
2. The first ten minutes: primitives and the handful of semantic colours
   that repaint the whole app; light and dark.
3. Contrast: what the warnings mean and how to clear one.
4. Type: uploading a font, what the app reads from it, licences, and that
   an unused font is left out at install.
5. Images and textures; that originals are kept in the draft and the
   installed theme carries WebP.
6. Going further with component tokens, and when to stop.
7. Custom CSS: hooks, what is and is not allowed, and scoping.
8. The specimen gallery and inspect mode: finding what styles a thing.
9. Calliope: the seven points in Spec J's implementation notes, and
   showing her a problem with a screenshot.
10. Finishing: the manifest, installing, the version bump, exporting, and
    sharing through a registry.
11. Working outside the app: `quilltap themes create`, `validate`,
    `install` and `export`.
