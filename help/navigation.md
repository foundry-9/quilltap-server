# Finding Your Way: The Rail and Its Menus

> **[Open this page in Quilltap](/)**

Down the left edge of every page runs the **rail** — a slim column of buttons that is, in effect, the house's switchboard. Ten buttons in all, and four of them open neat little menus, so that every room in the establishment is no more than two clicks away and every one of them has its name written plainly beside it.

When these pages tell you to **open Library and choose The Scriptorium**, they mean exactly that: press the **Library** button on the rail, and pick **The Scriptorium** from the menu that unfolds beside it.

## The Rail, Top to Bottom

From the top:

1. **Home** — the quill. Back to the front parlour.
2. **Characters** — your cast, in Aurora. One click; no menu.
3. **Conversations** — a menu (see below).
4. **Objects** — a menu.
5. **Library** — a menu.
6. **Studio** — a menu.

And at the foot, kept within a single click because they are what a lost or hurried visitor reaches for:

7. **Help** — the question mark, which opens the help chat.
8. **Quick-hide** — the eye, which folds sensitive things out of view at once.
9. **Settings** — the gear.
10. **Your profile picture** — a small menu holding **Profile** and **About**.

Rest the pointer on any rail button for its name. The button for whatever you are looking at wears a subtle mark, so you may always tell which menu the present page belongs to.

|---|---|

| **New Chat…** | Start a new conversation |

## Working the Menus

- **Click** a menu button to open it; click it again, press **Escape**, or click anywhere else to close it. Menus do not spring open on a mere hover — a wandering pointer will not ambush you.
- Only one menu is open at a time. Opening another closes the first.
- **From the keyboard**: Tab to a menu button and press **Enter**, **Space** or the **Right arrow** to open it. **Up** and **Down** move through the rows, **Home** and **End** leap to either end, and **Left** or **Escape** closes the menu and returns you to its button.
- A row you cannot use just now (the **Brahma Console** with no connection profile, say) stays in its menu, dimmed, with the reason written beneath it.
- Inside the workspace, a choice opens its page as a tab in place, so a conversation still being written is never interrupted. Middle-click, or Cmd/Ctrl-click, a page row to open it in a new browser tab instead.
- On a phone-width screen, a menu rises from the bottom of the screen as a sheet rather than unfolding beside the rail.

## In the Desktop App

The Quilltap desktop app keeps the rail exactly as it is, and — once its own update arrives — adds a menu bar along the top of the window with the very same names: **Conversations**, **Objects**, **Library** and **Studio**, plus a **Go** menu holding Home, Characters, Help, Quick-hide, Settings, Profile and About. Choosing from the menu bar does precisely what choosing from the rail does.
