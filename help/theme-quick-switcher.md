# Theme Quick-Switcher

> **[Open this page in Quilltap](/settings?section=appearance)**

The theme picker is the house's quickest way of changing frocks: a small menu that switches themes and colour modes (light, dark or system) at once, without a trip to Settings. It is always at hand, and there is no setting to enable or disable it.

## Opening the Picker

Open **Studio** and choose **Theme…**. A menu unfolds showing the themes available and, beneath them, the colour modes. It stays open until you choose something, click elsewhere, or press Escape.

## Selecting a Theme

The picker lists:

- **The default theme** — Always available, at the top
- **Bundled themes** — Six ship with Quilltap (Art Deco, Earl Grey, Great Estate, Madman's Box, Old School, Rains)
- **Installed bundle themes** — Any `.qtap-theme` bundles you have installed

Each entry shows colour swatches, the theme's name in its own heading font, a checkmark if it is the active one, and a dark-mode badge where the theme supports dark mode. Click any theme and the whole interface changes at once.

## Selecting a Colour Mode

Beneath the themes sit three colour modes:

- **Light** — Always use light mode (bright background)
- **Dark** — Always use dark mode (dark background)
- **System** — Follow your computer's preference (recommended)

Click one and the interface follows at once; the checkmark shows which is in force.

## The Picker or Settings?

Use **Studio → Theme…** when you want to switch quickly or experiment. Use the Appearance group of Settings (click **Settings** on the rail) when you want the fuller instruments: theme installation, the registry browser, and the rest. See [Appearance Settings](appearance-settings.md).

## Troubleshooting

### The Menu Won't Close

Click a theme or colour mode, click elsewhere on the page, or press Escape. If it is truly stuck, refresh the page.

### Themes Don't Change

Try clicking again, refresh the page, and check the browser console for errors.

### Wrong Colours Display

Check your colour mode (Light, Dark or System), make sure the intended theme is selected, and, if you use System mode, verify your computer's own light/dark preference.

## Your Preferences Are Saved

Your theme and colour-mode choices are saved to your account and persist across sessions.
