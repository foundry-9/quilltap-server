# Project Atlas

> **[Open this page in Quilltap](/prospero)**

A production has its fixtures: the ship the whole company sails in, the town it puts in at, the infernal engine in the hold. **Project Atlas** is where they are written down — an `Atlas/` folder in the project's own document store, consulted for every character seated in every chat of the project.

## Where to find it

On a project's page in Prospero, open the **Atlas** card. It shows how many pages the project keeps and opens the [Atlas](atlas.md) browser on the project's shelf. The same shelf may be chosen from the dropdown at the top of the Atlas anywhere in Quilltap, and the left rail's Atlas button opens on it from any chat of the project.

## What a project's pages are good for

- **The world of the chat.** An entry here is told to every character seated in the project's chats when it is named, and painted into the project's backgrounds when it is in the scene.
- **One shared truth.** Write the Convertible's appearance once, here, and every background and every `generate_image` in the project draws the same roadster.
- **A default a character may better.** A character with a page of the same name in their own vault is told theirs instead — the mechanic's notes on the engine, say, where the rest of the company knows only that it is fast.

## Things to know

- The project's [character roster](project-characters.md) is not consulted: the project's Atlas is the world, and everyone in it may know the world. (The roster governs what a character may *take* — files, the project wardrobe, the Inventory shelf. Nothing is taken here.)
- Everything else — names and other words, the graphic prompt and its gentle 200-character warning, what is known, pictures, folders, moving and copying between shelves, archiving — works exactly as described in [The Atlas](atlas.md).
