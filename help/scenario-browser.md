# The Scenario Browser — Every Scene on Every Shelf

> **[Open this page in Quilltap](/salon/new)**

The **Starting Scenario** drop-down is a discreet thing: it offers only the scenes belonging to *this* chat's household — the project's shelf, the shelves of any Group your company belongs to, the single character's own collection, and Quilltap General. That is usually exactly right. But now and then a scene written for one project, or tucked into one character's vault, is precisely the thing for an entirely different evening. For such occasions there is the browser.

## Opening It

In the scenario drop-down — on the New Chat form, or in the **Scenario** control of an open chat's **Chat** drawer — choose **Browse all scenarios…**, just beneath **Custom...**. Nothing is selected by choosing it; a dialog titled **Every scene on every shelf** opens instead.

## Finding a Scene

- **Every shelf at once.** On the left, the owners are sorted into four sections — **General**, **Projects**, **Groups** and **Characters** — each with a count of what it holds. A shelf with nothing on it appears greyed ("Nothing filed here yet."), so you know it is bare rather than wondering whether it was overlooked. Shelves this chat already offers carry a small **in this chat** tag.
- **Shelved by title.** Each shelf lists its scenarios by title, then filename, with a leading "The" ignored — *The Gilded Cage* sits among the Gs.
- **One shelf.** Click an owner to see only its scenes; **Every shelf** shows them all again.
- **Search.** The box at the top ("Search every shelf…") searches names, descriptions, the opening lines of each scene and the owner's name, across every shelf at once, whichever shelf is selected. Should nothing answer, the browser says so: *Nothing on that shelf answers to the name.*
- **Show archived** brings out the scenes under dust sheets, marked *archived*.
- **Preview.** Click a scene (or move through the list with the arrow keys) and its full text is read out beneath the list.

**Use this scenario** (or Enter, or a double-click) takes it.

## What the Drop-Down Shows Afterwards

A scene from one of this chat's own shelves simply appears in the drop-down under its usual heading, as though you had chosen it there. A scene from anywhere else appears under a heading of its own, **Elsewhere**, labelled *Owner › Name* — *Blackwood › The Reading Room*, say. Either way the scene's text is read at the moment the chat begins (or the scene is changed), and your notes are layered beneath it just as they are for any preset.

Only the four kinds of shelf are offered: character vaults, each project's own store, each Group's own store, and Quilltap General. Scenes kept in other Scriptorium stores are not.

## Where the Scene Came From

A chat now remembers which file its scene was read from. The **Scenario** control in the chat's sidebar names it — **From: *Owner* › *Name*** — and keeps an eye on the original:

- **"— the shelf copy has been revised"** means the file has been edited since the scene was picked. **Reload from source** reads it afresh, keeps your notes beneath it, and the Host announces the revision as for any change of scene. Until you press it, the chat keeps the scene it has — amending your notes and pressing **Change scenario** does not quietly bring in the new copy.
- **"— the shelf copy is gone"** means the file has been deleted or renamed. The chat's scene is unaffected; there is simply nothing to reload.

Chats begun before this remembering began, and scenes typed by hand, have no such line.
