# Homepage

> **[Open this page in Quilltap](/)**

The homepage is the front hall of the establishment: the place one passes through on the way to everything else, and where the house keeps, laid out in plain sight, the things a regular visitor reaches for first. It is arranged in bands, one beneath another, each exactly as tall as what it holds. Nothing is trimmed to fit the window; should the hall run longer than your screen, it scrolls, as a sensible hall ought.

## What You'll See

### The Greeting and Quick Actions

Across the top, on a single line, the house bids you welcome by name. Beside the greeting stand four buttons for beginning something new:

- **Start a Chat** — open a fresh conversation with a character of your choosing
- **Start Autonomous Room** — set characters conversing among themselves, on a schedule or on demand
- **New Project** — found a project to keep related conversations and files under one roof
- **Generate Image** — commission a picture from the Lantern

### Where You Left Off

The conversation you last attended is given pride of place, in a card of its own:

- **Its picture** — the conversation's story background, hung at a proper size. A conversation with no background shows the portraits of its cast instead
- **Its title, in full** — however long-winded; nothing is cut short
- **Who is in it**, and the project it is filed under, if any
- **A line on where matters stand** — drawn from the conversation's own running summary, once it has been going long enough to have one
- **Resume** — one button, and you are back in the room
- **The message count**, with the Concierge's mark beside it when the conversation wears one (see below)

If you have told Quick-hide to keep certain conversations out of sight, this card obeys: it shows the most recent conversation you are willing to have on display, never one you have hidden.

### Recent Conversations

Beside the resume card sit the half-dozen conversations before it, each in a row broad enough for its whole title (two lines, if it wants them). Every row shows:

- **A picture** — the story background, or the cast's portraits
- **The title**
- **The participants**, and the project the conversation belongs to
- **When it was last active**, and **how many messages** it runs to
- **The Concierge's mark** — A small asterisk beside the message count, worn
  only by a conversation on which the Concierge's default arrangement no longer
  stands. It comes in two shades: **red**, and the conversation is
  **Unmoderated** — sent to the uncensored desk, whether the Concierge took its
  measure and moved it there himself or you opened the door with your own hand;
  **grey**, and it is **Locked** to the usual desks by your word. A
  **Moderated** conversation, the ordinary case, wears no mark at all. Rest the pointer on any asterisk
  and he will explain himself at length, and tell you where the switch is kept.

Click any row to step back into that conversation. **View all** leads to the Salon's complete register.

A household with only one conversation to its name sees the resume card alone, stretched across the row; one with none at all is offered a button to begin the first.

### The Company

Next comes a wall of portraits: your characters, two rows deep, as many abreast as the window allows. Your **favorites** hang first, each with a small star at the corner, and after them the characters you call upon most.

- **Click a portrait** to start a new chat with that character
- **Rest the pointer on a portrait** (or reach it with the Tab key) and a small card opens with the character's title and the model they speak through, together with two further doors: **Resume** their most recent conversation, and **Open** their page in Aurora. Press **Escape** to shut the card
- **The last place on the wall** is reserved for everyone who did not fit; it leads to the full roster, as does **View all**

Characters you play yourself, and characters marked as NPCs, are not hung here.

### Projects

At the foot of the hall is the projects shelf: one tile per project, most recently active first, in as many rows as it takes. Each tile gives the project's name, how many conversations it holds, and when it last saw activity. Click a tile to open the project, or the small green button at its end to start a new chat inside it. Rest the pointer on a tile to read the project's description.

If you keep no projects, the shelf is simply not there, and the hall is no emptier for it. **New Project**, among the quick actions, will put it up.

## Navigation Tips

- Use the **rail** on the left to navigate between different sections of Quilltap
- Use the **search bar** at the top to quickly find chats, characters, or other content
- Use the **width toggle** button to adjust how much space the main content area takes up; a wider hall hangs more portraits abreast
- In a split workspace the bands answer to the width of their own pane: a narrow one stacks the resume card above the recent conversations

## Getting Started

1. **Create your first character** in Aurora, by way of the rail
2. **Start a chat** with your character to begin creating stories
3. **Organize your work** into projects to keep related content together
4. **Use the search bar** to quickly find anything across your workspace

Need help with a specific feature? Check the help resources for the rail, search, or other components.
